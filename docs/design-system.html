Principles

White page, one voice, one accent. Type does the work. Nothing is decorated. Every number is live and verifiable.

Always

  • Display type large, light weight, tight tracking
  • Grey secondary text inside the headline, never a second heading
  • Labels in Inter 500 at 13px, sentence case, normal tracking, muted
  • Hairlines and whitespace instead of cards
  • Exactly one dark block per page, for the statement that matters

Never

  • Gradients as decoration, glows, neon
  • A card around everything
  • Icons as section markers
  • Rounded-everything with drop shadows
  • Rockets, emojis, "to the moon"
  • Uppercase letter-spaced labels or a monospace label face — it reads as machine-generated

Voice

Short sentences. Plain words. Say where it runs and what it costs; never promise a return.

Say

  • "Trading fees become AI credits, every hour."
  • "Served by a Mac in the Mesh network."
  • "Your credits never expire."
  • "Credits are a share of fees, not a promise."

Never say

  • "Guaranteed yield", "passive income", "APY"
  • "Better than ChatGPT"
  • "Fully private" (say where it runs instead)
  • Anything with an exclamation mark
